SUBJECT: EMBARGO ON IMPORT OF 348 MILITARY SUBSYSTEMS, AVIONICS MODULES, AND SPECIALIZED AEROSPACE COMPONENTS TO ACCELERATE DOMESTIC DEFENCE PRODUCTION.

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
To foster self-reliance in defence manufacturing (Aatmanirbhar Bharat) and minimize foreign exchange drainage on critical aerospace sub-assemblies, the Ministry of Defence hereby notifies the 6th Positive Indigenisation List comprising 348 strategic hardware and software systems.

2. EMBARGO TIMELINES & RESTRICTED CATEGORIES:
- Category A (Effective 1st April 2026): High-power microwave tubes, digital active phased-array radar sub-modules, and airborne early warning mission computers.
- Category B (Effective 1st October 2026): Composite airframe fuselage sections, fly-by-wire flight control actuators, and tactical data-link transceivers.
- Category C (Effective 1st April 2027): Single-crystal turbine blade castings, electro-optical targeting pods, and submarine acoustic stealth coatings.

3. VALUE CREATION & CAPITAL ALLOCATION:
- Estimated Domestic Procurement Value: ₹68,500 Crores across Indian defence PSUs and private tier-1 aerospace suppliers over the next 5 fiscal years.
- Elimination of foreign technology license fee outflows resulting in an estimated 380 to 450 bps structural expansion in gross manufacturing margins for indigenous contractors.

4. PRIMARY RECIPIENTS OF CAPITAL ALLOCATION & CONTRACT AWARDS:
- Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (NSE: HAL): Primary integration authority for Tejas Mk1A, Mk2 fighter avionics, Light Combat Helicopter (Prachand) assemblies, and Sukhoi-30 MKI radar retrofits.
- Bharat Electronics Limited (NSE: BEL): Digital radar processors, electronic warfare (EW) suites, and secure tactical communications.
- Solar Industries India (NSE: SOLARINDS): Advanced energetic materials, warheads, and propellant casting.

5. QUALITY CERTIFICATION & VALIDATION PROTOCOL:
All indigenous replacement systems must obtain certification from the Directorate General of Aeronautical Quality Assurance (DGAQA) within 180 days of production sample delivery.
